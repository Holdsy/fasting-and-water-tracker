# Fasting and Water Tracker

## Overview
Welcome to the Fasting and Water Tracker app! This application is designed to help you monitor your fasting periods and water intake, ensuring you stay hydrated and maintain a healthy fasting regimen.

## Features
- **Fasting Tracker**: Easily track your fasting periods, with customizable start and end times.
- **Hydration Monitor**: Log and visualize your daily water intake, helping you stay on top of your hydration goals.
- **Reminders**: Set reminders for when to drink water or when your fasting period ends.
- **User-friendly Interface**: Enjoy a sleek and intuitive design, making it simple to navigate and use the app.
- **Analytics**: View your fasting and hydration statistics over time to analyze your progress.

## Technologies Used
- **Programming Language**: Swift
- **Frameworks**: SwiftUI for the user interface, Combine for reactive programming.

## Installation
To install the app, clone the repository and open the project in Xcode:
```bash
git clone https://github.com/Holdsy/fasting-and-water-tracker.git
cd fasting-and-water-tracker
open FastingAndWaterTracker.xcodeproj
```

## Usage
1. **Start a Fast**: Navigate to the Fasting Tracker and tap on "Start Fast" to begin.
2. **Log Water Intake**: Use the Hydration Monitor to log each glass of water consumed.
3. **Set Reminders**: Go to the settings to configure reminders based on your preferences.
4. **View Analytics**: Check the statistics section to view your progress in fasting and hydration.
